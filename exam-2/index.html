FAQs

How does URL shortening work?

URL shortening works by taking a long URL and creating a shorter, condensed version that redirects to the original URL. When a user clicks on the shortened link, they are redirected to the intended destination.

Is it necessary to create an account to use the URL shortening service?

No, you can shorten links without an account. Creating a free account lets you save, edit and track all of your links in one place.

Are the shortened links permanent? Will they expire?

Shortened links are permanent by default. You can choose to set an expiry date on any link from your dashboard.

Are there any limitations on the number of URLs I can shorten?

Every plan, including the free Basic plan, comes with unlimited URL shortening.

Can I customize the shortened URLs to reflect my brand or content?

Yes. Add a custom alias to any link, and on the Professional plan connect your own branded domain.

Can I track the performance of my shortened URLs?

Yes. Our analytics show clicks, locations, devices and referrers for each of your links and QR codes.

How secure is the URL shortening service? Are the shortened links protected against spam or malicious activity?

All links are served over HTTPS and scanned for spam and malware, and abusive links are disabled automatically.

What is a QR code and what can it do?

A QR code is a scannable image that opens a link on a phone camera. Use it on posters, packaging and menus to bring people straight to your content.

Is there an API available for integrating the URL shortening service into my own applications or websites?

Yes. The Teams plan includes API access so you can create and manage links from your own apps.
